QUICK GUIDE

Common questions

Which sound is best for sleep?

Brown noise, steady fan noise, rain and wind are popular starting points. Listen at a comfortable, non-intrusive volume.

Which sound is best for focus?

Try pink, green and white noise. The most useful option is usually the one that masks your environment while drawing the least attention to itself.

How loud should background noise be?

Keep it comfortably low—enough to soften distractions, never loud enough to feel tiring or overpowering.
