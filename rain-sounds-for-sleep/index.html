A quieter kind of weather

Gentle rain is one of the most familiar background environments because it has continuous movement without a fixed beat, lyric or story. This three-hour session places the listener on a sheltered rainforest terrace, where the rain is close enough to form a steady layer while the setting remains calm rather than dramatic. It is made for long, low-volume listening: no splashy transitions, surprise narration or music cues to break the spell once you have settled.

Use rain sound when you want a sense of place as well as basic sound masking. It can suit reading, a late-night work block, a sleep routine or a quiet period after a busy day. The rain will not suit everyone—some people prefer an abstract noise color, especially if nature recordings draw their attention. The most useful approach is to test the sound while doing the actual activity, not just while evaluating it for a few seconds.

Falling asleep

A low, continuous rain layer can make a room feel more consistent while you wind down.

Late study

Relaxation

The sheltered setting offers a gentle sense of place without the drama of a heavy storm.

Rain or noise color?

Rain brings small natural variations, while pink noise and brown noise remain more abstract and even. If you enjoy rain but want a little more weather presence, the city rain and distant thunder session adds a distant storm layer. For a more general overview, see our rain sounds for sleep guide.

Gentle rain questions

Does this rain sound include thunder?

This release focuses on gentle rain. For a storm recording with distant thunder, choose the city-rain soundscape.

Can I use rain sounds while studying?

Yes, if the natural variation feels less distracting than silence or music. Keep the level low enough that it stays in the background.
